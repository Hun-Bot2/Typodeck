## Korean preview rules

- Build exactly one title slide at 1920×1080, `lang="ko"`, fixed-stage model.
- Manual title in Pretendard 800, 92px, 1.14 leading, NO caps, keep-all + balance.
- Body in Pretendard 400, 1.65 leading, keep-all; dotted-grid paper unchanged.
- Doc codes / step codes / figure tags stay Latin caps mono, nowrap.
- Warning chips: Korean at 600 weight, widened chip, amber contrast kept.
- Load: Pretendard Variable (jsDelivr) + parent Archivo/Plex faces.
- Never place workflow text, slug, or template name on the slide.
