## Visual Snapshot

A Y2K chrome-and-candy presentation system built on an ice-blue ground (#E8F4FF) with glossy gradient bands in aqua, lilac, and bubblegum. Display type runs in Baloo 2 — a plump rounded sans — with headlines that layer color word by word (indigo base, bubblegum-pink emphasis, aqua highlight spans), while Quicksand carries body copy in a soft geometric voice. Content floats on white bubble cards with chunky rounded corners, indigo keylines, and specular gloss highlights; star-glyph sparkles scatter across the margins like screen shine. The effect sits between a candy store and a tomorrowland OS splash screen — unmistakably cheerful, deliberately shiny, and engineered to feel like a fresh boot into a friendlier future.

Bubble OS is a Y2K chrome-and-candy presentation system. Its foundational premise is the bubble: every container is a glossy rounded card, every label is a pill, every headline mixes color word by word, and every surface carries a specular highlight plus a scatter of star sparkles. Layouts feel inflated and buoyant — chrome gradients, candy pills, and shine marks keep the whole deck floating.

## Preview Ingredients

- Palette: ice-blue #E8F4FF; deep-indigo #1B1F6B; bubblegum #FF7AD9; aqua #7AF0FF; lilac #B388FF; card white #FFFFFF
- Typography: See full design doc after selection.
- Signature move: Layered color-word headlines in Baloo 2 — indigo base words, one bubblegum emphasis word, one aqua highlight span — never a single flat color at display scale.
- Signature move: White bubble cards with 40px+ radii, 4px indigo keylines, and a specular gloss wash across the top edge.
- Signature move: Glossy gradient bands (aqua → lilac → bubblegum) bookending the slide top and bottom with rounded inner corners.
- Signature move: Pill badges for every label — indigo, bubblegum, aqua, and lilac pills with inset top-shine and a soft indigo drop.
- Signature move: Star-glyph sparkles (★) scattered at mixed sizes and rotations in the margins — the system's shine mark.
- Signature move: Dashed indigo divider rules and chunky page-number pills instead of hairlines or plain folios.
