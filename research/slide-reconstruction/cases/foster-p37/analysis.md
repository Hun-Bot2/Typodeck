# foster-p37 — Centered monumental quote (NEW pattern)

Source: Gardiner/Ada Initiative, Wikimania 2012 keynote (CC BY-SA 3.0), p37.
Six-line centered slab-bold quote (~38–40pt EST) with curly quotes + centered
footer. Quote fills the middle field; red flourish rises behind the footer.
Substitutions: bg → solid black; slab → Archivo Black; single 38pt block with
natural wrap (source breaks are incidental wraps, not forced — size fidelity
prioritized over break fidelity; HTML/PPTX wraps may differ, recorded).
Color #E6E6E6 EST (brighter than p15 body).
