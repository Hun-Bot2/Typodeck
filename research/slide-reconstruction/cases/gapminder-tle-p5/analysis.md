# Analysis: gapminder-tle-p5 (pilot)

Source: Gapminder Foundation, "Test Question 3: World Life
Expectancy" p5 (CC-BY 4.0). Neighbor role: explanation card after
the answer reveal; dense "why ignorance?" explainer.

## Canvas

- 720×540 pt = 4:3 (capture 1500×1125). White field.

## Hierarchy (top to bottom, left-aligned)

1. Running head `TEST QUESTION: WORLD LIFE EXPECTANCY` + `Q3.1` —
   micro caps, pale blue-gray, full-width row.
2. Headline `WHY IGNORANCE?` — bold caps, mustard, ~2× body EST.
3. Lede paragraph — regular slate, 4 lines, measure ~85% width.
4. Three subhead+body groups (`Outdated fact`, `Macro Trends are
   not news worthy`, `Exaggeration of extremes`) — bold slate
   subheads + regular body; small yellow icon bullets (decorative,
   omitted in reconstruction).
5. Closing paragraph + underlined URL (link styling noted; PPTX
   keeps text only, no hyperlink object).
6. Standard footer microline (same 3-zone template as tq2-p1).

## Type and geometry (EST)

- Category: humanist sans throughout, 2 weights + caps kickers.
- Alignment: hard left edge (x ≈ 5% EST); running head spans width.
- Rhythm: headline→lede tight; generous gaps between groups
  (≈ 1× body size); footer pinned bottom.
- Density HIGH (only dense case in pilot): ~180 words + footer.
- Contrast: slate-on-white high; mustard headline medium-high.

## Reconstruction notes

- Icon bullets omitted (decorative); subhead weight carries grouping.
- Stress test for Typodeck text measure + vertical rhythm at density.
- 4:3→16:9 migration: measure grows; watch orphan lines (honest risk).
