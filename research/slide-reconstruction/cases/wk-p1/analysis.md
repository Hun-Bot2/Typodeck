# Analysis: wk-p1 (pilot)

Source: Whose Knowledge?, "Decolonising Wikidata" keynote, WikidataCon
2021 (CC BY-SA 4.0, Commons own-work). Neighbor role: deck opener;
followed by shout-out/section cards.

## Canvas

- 720×405 pt = 16:9 (capture 1500×844). Direct same-dimension
  comparison with Typodeck output (13.33×7.5in).

## Hierarchy (top to bottom, centered)

1. Title `Decolonising Wikidata` — bold condensed sans, white, 1 line,
   ~12% canvas height EST.
2. Subtitle `WHY DOES KNOWLEDGE JUSTICE MATTER / FOR STRUCTURED DATA?`
   — letterspaced caps, smaller, white, 2 lines.
3. Attribution `Anasuya Sengupta / Co-founder and co-director, /
   Whose Knowledge?` — small regular, white, 3 lines.
4. Corner marks: `Whose Knowledge?` logo (bottom-left), `WIKIDATACON
   2021` badge (bottom-right) — white, small.
5. Background: dark navy abstract line pattern (decorative, not type;
   reconstruction substitutes solid dark field per typography-only).

## Type and geometry (EST)

- Category: condensed grotesque (title), same family caps (subtitle).
- Relative sizes: title ≈ 2.2× subtitle ≈ 4× attribution.
- Alignment: centered stack; optical center slightly above geometric.
- Vertical rhythm: title→subtitle gap ≈ 0.6× subtitle size;
  subtitle→attribution gap ≈ 1.5× attribution size (breathing gap).
- Margins: generous; side whitespace ≈ 20% each; corner marks inset ≈ 3%.
- No cropping; density low-moderate.
- Contrast: white-on-navy (very high).

## Reconstruction notes

- Pattern bg → solid dark (documented substitution; fidelity cost: low,
  background carries no semantic content).
- Corner marks → two caption blocks (honest: logo redrawn as type).
