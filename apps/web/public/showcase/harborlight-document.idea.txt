Harborlight is a weekend ferry booking companion for coastal commuters. The problem: ferry schedules live in PDFs and station posters, so riders miss sailings and queue twice. Harborlight pulls the published timetable into a readable trip board, sends a nudge 20 minutes before boarding, and keeps an offline copy of your ticket. The crew dashboard shows live headcounts per sailing so staff can open a second gate before crowds build. We charge operators a flat monthly fee per vessel, starting with the 12-boat fleet in our home harbor. This season we aim to cut missed boardings by half and grow weekly riders from 400 to 1500.
