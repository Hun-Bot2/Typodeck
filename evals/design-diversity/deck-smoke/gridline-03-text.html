Finding · Review Discipline

Three patterns in every rushed postmortem

  1. 01

    Configuration ships unreviewed

    Flags, limits, and secrets change outside the diff. Eight of eleven outages began in a file no reviewer opened.

  2. 02

    Approval becomes ceremony

    Two-click sign-offs under deadline pressure. Median review time fell from 41 minutes to 6 before failures.

  3. 03

    Rollbacks lack a rehearsed path

    Teams knew the forward plan, not the way back. Recovery took days where a practiced rollback needed minutes.
