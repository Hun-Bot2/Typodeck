Chapter Three

Three Patterns in Every Rushed Postmortem

◆

First, configuration ships unreviewed: flags, limits, and secrets change outside the diff, and eight of eleven outages began in a file no reviewer opened.

Second, approval becomes ceremony: two-click sign-offs under deadline pressure, with median review time falling from forty-one minutes to six before failures.

Third, rollbacks lack a rehearsed path: teams knew the forward plan but not the way back, and recovery took days where a practiced rollback needed minutes.
