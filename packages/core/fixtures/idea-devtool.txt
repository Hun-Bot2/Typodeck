Shipgate is a deployment CLI for small teams that dread release Fridays. Every release used to need 14 manual checklist steps, and a missed step once took down checkout for 47 minutes on 2026-09-15.

Shipgate turns the checklist into a single reviewed file: version 1.4.2 runs 22 preflight checks in about 9 seconds and blocks the deploy on any failure. The old YAML format is deprecated and will be removed in 2.0, after commit a91c4e2 migrated the last team off it.

Previously the founders shelled out to bash scripts that only one person understood. The roadmap will add progressive rollouts in phase two and one-click rollbacks that restore service in under 60 seconds.

Pilot teams report calm releases: failed deploys dropped 80 percent across 300 releases, and onboarding takes 11 minutes from install to first green deploy.
