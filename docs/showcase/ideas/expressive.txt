Fieldnotes Live is a three-day outdoor storytelling festival on the Oregon coast. Forty speakers, one cliffside stage, and 1,200 attendees who camp under the pines and trade stories until the fog burns off. The 2026 edition runs August 14 to 16 at Oswald West State Park, with headliners from public radio, expedition filmmaking, and long-form print.

Each day holds a single arc. Mornings open with a 90-minute live interview. Afternoons split into six field workshops capped at 40 seats each, from analog recording to tide-pool sketching. Evenings close with a 70-minute main-stage session under string lights, followed by an open-mic fire circle that has run past midnight all four previous years.

Tickets are 240 dollars for the full weekend, including campsite access and two communal dinners. Last year sold out in eleven days with 92 percent of attendees rating the weekend nine or higher out of ten. This year adds a second Dawn Chorus walk, a print anthology of the ten best audience stories, and a scholarship fund covering 60 tickets for student storytellers. The festival is produced by a six-person crew with 45 local volunteers and a leave-no-trace record of four straight years.
