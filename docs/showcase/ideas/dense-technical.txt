Meridian Edge 2.0 architecture review. The gateway fleet runs 340 nodes across 6 regions, each node a 16-core ARM instance with 64 gigabytes of RAM and a 2-terabyte NVMe volume. Ingress terminates TLS 1.3 at the edge, enforces mutual TLS between services, and routes to 48 stateless API workers over HTTP/2 with a 250-millisecond budget per hop and a 3-second end-to-end deadline.

Request path. Edge proxy validates JWT signatures against a JWKS cache refreshed every 5 minutes, applies a token-bucket rate limit of 2,000 requests per minute per key with a burst allowance of 400, then forwards to the router. The router matches 212 registered routes, strips hop-by-hop headers, injects a trace context with a 128-bit trace id, and selects a worker by least-loaded scheduling recomputed every 10 seconds. Workers execute handlers with a 900-millisecond soft timeout, emit structured logs with trace and span ids, and record histograms for latency at the 50th, 95th, and 99th percentiles.

Data layer. Primary store is a 9-node cluster with 3 replicas per shard, synchronous replication within a region and asynchronous cross-region with a 4-second lag target. Hot cache holds 120 gigabytes in memory with a 92 percent hit ratio and a 300-second default TTL. Object storage retains 14 months of audit events, roughly  billions of rows, partitioned by day and queryable within 8 seconds for a 24-hour window. Nightly snapshots complete in 47 minutes and restore drills run every quarter with a 2-hour recovery objective and a 15-minute recovery point objective.

Reliability record. Over the trailing 90 days the platform served 4.1 billion requests at 99.97 percent availability, with 11 minutes of partial degradation during the March deploy and zero data-loss incidents. The 95th-percentile latency is 184 milliseconds and the 99th is 610 milliseconds, both measured at the edge. Autoscaling adds capacity in 90-second increments when CPU exceeds 70 percent for 3 consecutive minutes, and load tests at 3 times peak traffic pass with 38 percent headroom. On-call carries 6 engineers across 3 time zones, a 5-minute acknowledge target, and 212 runbook pages covering 74 alert rules. The review proposes five changes: raise the per-hop budget to 300 milliseconds, add a second JWKS fallback, partition the audit store by hour, extend snapshots to 18 months, and fund two additional edge regions in 2027.
